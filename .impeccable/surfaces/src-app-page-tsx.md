## Direction contract

THESIS: A black-and-white studio page where colour only comes from Ivan's work. Refuses the category default of a dark hero with tagline + buttons + equal project cards (the previous version).

OWN-WORLD: pure black #000 and white #fff fields that swap per section, ink #0a0a0a, one grey #6b6b6b for secondary text on white only. PP Mori Regular for everything, large statements at weight 400 with tight tracking, no accent colour, no gradients, no glass, no pills except the cursor badge. Images square-cornered, 4px max.

STORY: welcome stack of his work counts to 99 and collapses into the hero stack; a reviewer reads two plain lines, sees the work fan out, scrolls into a curved WebGL ribbon of case studies that bends with scroll speed, sees the visual archive scattered asymmetrically, three service rows, an about statement, and a giant "Let's talk" email close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: black. Band nav (wordmark, Work / Services / About, EN/ID + Email). Two statements at ~2.4rem: "Interfaces that work." top-left, "Visuals that get remembered." top-right. Centre: a tight deck of 7 work cards (front card left at --frame-w, the rest stepping back to the right, after avecanni's hero film) with a small "ivan ghazali" on top in mix-blend-difference. Disciplines mid-left, live Semarang clock mid-right. Primary action: Email in the nav and in the close.

FORM: owner-pinned composite (avecanni structure + Set 3 preloader + Set 2 ribbon). Seed key 21f051ae was rolled before the owner pinned the direction and is superseded.

Signature interaction: the welcome's last frame lands on the deck's front card; then a ~12s live loop (owner-chosen "shuffle deck", 2026-10-03): the front card slides out and tucks in at the back three times, the deck collapses, the name rises letter by letter at full width, shrinks back to the centre, the deck grows again. Pauses off-screen, static for reduced motion. The ribbon section pins while vertical scroll drives it sideways and scroll velocity deepens the paper curve.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
